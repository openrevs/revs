Import

Merge a ratingset into your collection: a signed sjsonl file, or an unsigned one such as a revs tui draft.jsonl. Nothing is overwritten or deleted. Movies and TV series are matched by ID or by title and year (a movie never matches a series), and viewings you already have are skipped.

Import CSV

Add movies from a CSV file, such as one exported from a spreadsheet. The first line must be a header naming the columns. Supported columns are title (required), year, rating (½–{{.MaxStars}} stars, halves allowed, e.g. 3.5), view_date (YYYY-MM-DD), imdb_id, imdb_id_no_tt (an IMDb ID without the leading tt, as some exports write it), tmdb_id and review (free text; quote it to include commas or line breaks); other columns are ignored. Each row is a movie, and a row with a rating, view date or review also adds a viewing. Movies are matched and merged the same way as above, and if any row is invalid nothing is imported.
